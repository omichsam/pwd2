FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about the disability certification process.

Any Kenyan citizen with a documented disability can apply for certification. Medical professionals at our partner hospitals will assess your condition during the appointment.
Typically 3-4 weeks from registration to final certification. This includes hospital appointment, medical review, health officer approval, and county certification. Some cases may take longer depending on the complexity of the assessment.
The certification process is free for all PWD citizens. You only pay if you require additional medical tests or services beyond the standard assessment.
Each certificate comes with a unique QR code. Scan the code or visit our verification portal to instantly confirm if a certificate is genuine and active.
